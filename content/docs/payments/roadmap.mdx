---
title: Roadmap
description: Where the payments library is headed — drivers hardening, splits & subaccounts, dashboard and more invoice coverage.
---

- **Invoice providers** — Focus, eNotas, PlugNotas, Asaas native NFS-e and Tecnospeed ship
  today (or [write your own](./custom-providers) with the exported building blocks)
- **BR drivers hardening** — AbacatePay transparent checkout (PIX/Boleto) and Woovi Pix
  Automático journey 3 (pay-on-approval, `DYNAMIC`) are shipped
- **Webhook business logic** — the three options (folder, `billing.handlers`, events) are
  shipped; next: an out-of-the-box `@OnDiagnostic` durable trigger example once the
  durable event-trigger release lands
- **Commands** — `payments:webhook --create` (Stripe) and `payments:sync --all`
  (reconcile every customer) are shipped
- **Usage-based billing** — metered subscriptions shipped (`billing_usage_events`,
  `recordUsage`/`usageReport`, and the period **metered bill** via `meteredBill`); next:
  auto-invoice the metered amount
- **Dashboard** — headless `billingOverview` data foundation shipped (revenue, active subscriptions, usage per meter); next: the dashboard SPA (Agora dashboard pattern)
- **Splits & subaccounts** — Asaas split and Woovi subaccounts are shipped
